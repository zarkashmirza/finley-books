Our Story

At Finley Books, we believe the best stories are built from the ground up. Our doors opened with a simple mission: to provide a sanctuary where the raw mechanics of writing and the joy of reading intersect. We are more than just a place to buy books; we are a dedicated space for literary development, championing everything from tightly woven short stories to sweeping, long-form narratives.

Our founders understood that true creativity requires a focused, distraction-free environment. That is why our shelves are curated to inspire those who appreciate the unadorned beauty of plain text and the quiet discipline of drafting. The wandering figure in our logo serves as our emblem -- a reminder that every reader and writer is on a continuous journey of discovery, moving purposefully toward their next chapter.

Whether you are hunting for a rare edition, seeking a quiet corner to edit your latest manuscript, or simply looking to connect with a community that respects the structural craft of storytelling, you have found your place. Step into Finley Books, find your focus, and let the narrative take over.

SPOTLIGHT: Meet Michael Lewis

This month, the Finley Books spotlight shines on our star author, Michael Lewis. By day (and most evenings), Michael is a professor at TriOS College, where he brings a remarkably good-natured and humorous approach to his students and his curriculum. By night, he is a storyteller and creator who just released his highly anticipated new release, Centering A Div: A Memoir -- a witty, reflective journey that anyone who has ever wrestled with web layouts (or life's stubborn alignments) will deeply appreciate. When he is not grading assignments or writing his next chapter, Michael spends his free time producing his own music, and has enjoyed tremendous success under his pseudonym, "DAKUMA." You can check out his YouTube Channel, Chilldeck, where his greatest hits live. Michael is truly a testament to the idea that the best narratives -- and the best melodies -- come from those who never stop creating.
